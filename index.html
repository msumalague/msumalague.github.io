I'm Mairene Sumalague, a machine learning engineer and software engineer who turns data into products people can depend on, from production AI to the web and mobile apps around it. I lead machine learning delivery for production platforms, designing classification and regression models, building generative AI and agentic workflows, and engineering the pipelines, retraining jobs, and dashboards that keep them reliable over time.

My path into engineering started early. My school did not offer programming, so I taught myself HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in junior high and later picked up Java and Python on my own. Computer Engineering at Batangas State University then gave me both ends of the stack, from microprocessors and digital logic to software, data, and applied AI. That foundation still shapes how I work, and I am as comfortable tuning a model as I am building a React interface or a Flutter app, or wiring a Raspberry Pi or NVIDIA Jetson into a prototype.

I value clear ownership, fast learning, and systems that are dependable, well documented, and easy for a team to maintain. Away from the keyboard, I am usually planning my next trip, most often to Japan or China, which is where the neon skyline on this page comes from. The cat projected into that skyline is Kopicat, my own cat, named for his coffee latte coat and reimagined here as a cyborg hologram.

How I work: the full ML lifecycle

I take ownership of models from the first look at the data through to monitoring in production. Each stage informs the next, and what monitoring reveals feeds back into the start.

  1. AnalyzeExplore data and frame the problem
  2. PrepareClean, join, and validate inputs
  3. EngineerBuild and select features
  4. Train & evaluateCompare models against clear metrics
  5. ServeRun inference behind APIs and apps
  6. MonitorTrack freshness, performance, and alerts
